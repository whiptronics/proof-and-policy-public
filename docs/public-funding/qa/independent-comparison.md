## Federal reimbursement for one free lunch, SY2024-25

89 FR 56720: Section 11 $4.01 plus Section 4 $0.42 = $4.43 in contiguous states (school food authorities under 60% free and
reduced-price), +$0.02 at 60% or above, +$0.09 if certified for the performance-based payment. A reimbursement rate, not a cost per meal; no second source
was read for it.
